# Customer Evidence & Voice-of-Customer Governance

## Purpose

Protect customer research, Voice-of-Customer (VOC), customer-language, segmentation, and competitor-marketing research from turning limited observations into universal customer truths.

## Core Rule

**Observed customer language is evidence about the observed sample, not automatically evidence about the whole market.**

A specialist must distinguish:

- **Direct quote** — verbatim customer language with source attribution.
- **Observation** — pattern directly present in the collected material.
- **Frequency** — count derived from the actual dataset; state denominator when meaningful.
- **Inference** — interpretation of observed patterns.
- **Hypothesis** — explanation that still needs validation.
- **Benchmark** — external comparison, clearly labeled and sourced.
- **Recommendation** — proposed action based on evidence and diagnosis.

## Sampling Discipline

Do not treat an arbitrary interview/review count, interview duration, persona count, research cadence, or sample composition as a universal requirement. Such numbers may be useful planning defaults, but must be labeled as examples or benchmarks unless the methodology specifically justifies them.

Always state important limitations such as:

- source type and provenance
- sample size or available volume
- time period
- segment/role/geography represented
- missing or underrepresented groups
- self-selection or survivorship bias when relevant

## VOC Rules

- Never fabricate customer quotes.
- Never turn a paraphrase into quotation marks.
- Preserve meaningful customer wording when quoting.
- Do not let the most frequent phrase automatically become the most strategically important issue; frequency and importance are separate dimensions.
- Do not infer willingness to pay, purchase intent, market size, or causal impact from language alone.
- Do not generalize one review, one interview, one social comment, or one sales call to the whole market.
- Conflicting customer signals should be surfaced rather than averaged away.

## Research Design Boundary

Customer research specialists own research design, interviewing, synthesis, and evidence quality. They do **not** independently decide the company's final positioning, target market, channel strategy, pricing, or business strategy.

Strategic Intelligence remains the decision layer. If customer evidence materially changes the diagnosis, audience definition, strategic priority, or recommended intervention, return the evidence and conflict to Strategic Intelligence before downstream execution.

## Competitor-Marketing Research

Observing that a competitor uses a channel, message, cadence, offer, or tactic does not prove that the tactic causes its performance. Separate:

**Observed behavior → inferred mechanism → hypothesis about effectiveness.**

Do not invent competitor revenue, conversion, CAC, audience size, performance, or customer outcomes.

## Output Quality Gate

Before finalizing customer evidence work, verify:

- Can every quote be traced to a source?
- Are counts reproducible from the available material?
- Are sample limitations explicit?
- Are observations separated from interpretations?
- Are conflicting signals visible?
- Are strategic conclusions proportional to evidence strength?
- Would the conclusion still be valid if the sample were narrower than the market?
